## nextLEVEL® Value Creation Plan
Focus: Growth Operators' proprietary assessment and transformation framework, designed to identify operational gaps, benchmark business functions against best practices, prioritize improvement opportunities, and develop actionable value creation roadmaps.

Assessment Areas:
- Finance & Accounting
- Human Resources
- Strategic IT
- Decision Intelligence & AI

Framework:
- Evaluates 27 capabilities against 300 criteria.
- Uses a five-point scoring system to assess organizational performance.
- Benchmarks functional capabilities against established best practices.
- Identifies operational inefficiencies, risks, and improvement opportunities.
- Produces customized scorecards and prioritized recommendations.
- Helps leadership teams determine which initiatives will create the greatest business value.

Deliverables:
- Comprehensive operational assessment
- Functional performance scorecards
- Gap analysis and improvement priorities
- Customized value creation roadmap
- 100-day action plan
- One-year improvement plan
- Multi-year transformation roadmap
- Implementation support from experienced Growth Operators practitioners

The nextLEVEL® framework can be applied across multiple business functions or tailored to a specific functional area. It provides the foundation for Growth Operators' hands-on advisory and business transformation engagements.
