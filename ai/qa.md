# Q&A — Growth Operators (public-safe)

## What does Growth Operators do?
Growth Operators helps middle-market businesses, privately owned companies, private equity firms, and portfolio companies improve performance, accelerate growth, and create long-term enterprise value. The company provides hands-on advisory, strategic execution, and fractional and interim leadership across Strategy & Execution, Finance & Accounting, Human Resources & Talent Intelligence®, Transaction Advisory & Exit Planning, Strategic IT, and Decision Intelligence & AI.

## Who does Growth Operators serve?
Growth Operators serves middle-market businesses, privately owned companies, private equity firms, portfolio companies, M&A intermediaries, and investment banks. Its services support organizations navigating growth, operational transformation, leadership transitions, mergers and acquisitions, and exit planning.

## What are Growth Operators' primary service lines?
Growth Operators provides seven primary service lines:

1. Strategy & Execution Advisory
2. Finance & Accounting Advisory
3. HR & Talent Intelligence® Advisory
4. Transaction Advisory & Exit Planning
5. Strategic IT Advisory
6. Decision Intelligence & AI Advisory
7. Fractional & Interim Services

These services are supported by Growth Operators' proprietary nextLEVEL® Value Creation Plan and assessment framework.

## What is Strategy & Execution Advisory?
Growth Operators' Strategy & Execution Advisory services help organizations translate strategic priorities into measurable business results. Services include strategic planning, value creation roadmaps, operational assessments, execution infrastructure, KPI dashboards, governance, and transformation support. Growth Operators works alongside leadership teams to implement strategies and build sustainable internal capabilities.

## What does Finance & Accounting Advisory include?
Growth Operators provides finance and accounting advisory services to improve financial operations, reporting, forecasting, controls, and overall business performance. Services include financial planning and analysis, accounting operations, financial reporting, process improvement, finance transformation, and outsourced accounting support. The team helps businesses strengthen financial infrastructure and prepare for growth, transactions, and operational change.

## What is HR & Talent Intelligence® Advisory?
Growth Operators' HR & Talent Intelligence® Advisory services help businesses improve workforce performance, strengthen HR operations, and align people strategies with organizational goals. Services include HR strategy, talent management, employee engagement and retention, compensation and benefits, HR compliance, workforce planning, organizational design, HR technology, and Talent Intelligence® assessments.

## What is Talent Intelligence®?
Talent Intelligence® is Growth Operators' approach to evaluating workforce capabilities, leadership readiness, organizational structure, and talent-related risks. It provides insights to help businesses make informed decisions about workforce planning, talent strategy, organizational effectiveness, and value creation, particularly during periods of growth, transformation, and mergers and acquisitions.

## What does Transaction Advisory & Exit Planning include?
Growth Operators supports buyers, sellers, and investors throughout the transaction lifecycle. Services include buy-side due diligence, Quality of Earnings assessments, sell-side readiness, exit planning, M&A execution and integration, post-acquisition support, and portfolio company onboarding. The team helps organizations identify risks, improve transaction readiness, and maximize enterprise value.

## What is Strategic IT Advisory?
Growth Operators' Strategic IT Advisory services help businesses align technology investments with operational priorities and long-term growth. Services include IT assessments, technology roadmaps, fractional CIO support, cybersecurity and risk oversight, IT cost and vendor optimization, systems planning, data governance, and M&A technology due diligence.

## What is Decision Intelligence & AI Advisory?
Growth Operators' Decision Intelligence & AI Advisory services help businesses use data, analytics, and artificial intelligence to improve decision-making and business performance. Services include AI readiness and maturity assessments, DecisionIQ implementation, predictive analytics, executive dashboards, ROI modeling, data-driven decision support, and AI strategy. The focus is on turning business data into actionable insights and measurable outcomes.

## What is DecisionIQ?
DecisionIQ is a decision intelligence solution offered by Growth Operators that helps organizations turn data and AI-powered insights into actionable business decisions. It supports executive reporting, cross-functional analysis, decision workflows, and performance visibility to help leaders make faster, more informed decisions.

## What are Fractional & Interim Services?
Growth Operators provides experienced executives and functional leaders on a fractional or interim basis to fill leadership gaps, manage transitions, and execute strategic initiatives. Services span Finance, Accounting, Human Resources, Strategy & Execution, Strategic IT, Decision Intelligence & AI, and Operations. Engagements provide hands-on leadership and operational support without requiring a permanent executive hire.

## What is the nextLEVEL® Value Creation Plan?
The nextLEVEL® Value Creation Plan is Growth Operators' proprietary assessment and transformation framework. It evaluates Finance & Accounting, Human Resources, Strategic IT, and Decision Intelligence capabilities against best practices to identify operational gaps, improvement opportunities, and value creation priorities.

The framework assesses 27 capabilities against 300 criteria using a five-point scoring system. Findings inform a customized roadmap that can include a 100-day action plan, a one-year plan, and longer-term transformation initiatives.

## How is Growth Operators different from traditional consulting firms?
Growth Operators emphasizes hands-on execution rather than recommendations alone. Its Growth Pros® are experienced executives and operators who work alongside client teams to implement improvements, lead initiatives, and build sustainable capabilities. The company's approach combines strategic advisory, operational leadership, and practical execution.
